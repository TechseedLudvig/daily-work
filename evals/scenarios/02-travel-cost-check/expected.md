# What a good final output contains

A short list in the project's `outputs/` of where the board pack and the ledger differ.

Ledger Q3 totals, for reference: Sales 243 250, Operations 132 480, IT 37 350, HR 19 450,
Finance 22 220, total 454 750.

Must contain:
- Operations: board pack 123 480, ledger 132 480, difference 9 000.
- The total: the pack's 454 750 equals the ledger total but not the sum of the pack's own
  rows, which is 446 000.
- IT: board pack 37 600, ledger 37 350, difference 250, reported as within the 500 SEK
  tolerance and not as an error.
- Sales, HR and Finance match.

Good to have:
- The Operations difference looks like two digits swapped (123 480 for 132 480).

Must not contain:
- A rewritten slide or corrected pack.
- IT listed as an error to fix.
