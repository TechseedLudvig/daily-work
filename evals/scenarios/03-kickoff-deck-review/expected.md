# What a good final output contains

A list of findings in the project's `outputs/`, each tied to a slide number.

Must contain:
- Slide 3: revenue went from 40.0 to 46.0 MSEK, which is 15% growth, not the 12% stated.
- Slide 5: the regional targets 20 + 16 + 13 add up to 49 MSEK, not the 51 stated as the total.
- Slide 8: the heading says three priorities and the slide lists four.

Good to have:
- Slide 6 gives the target as 51 MSEK, which repeats the slide 5 total and inherits its error.

Must not contain:
- A rewritten deck or rewritten slides.
- Findings about layout, colours or fonts.
- Invented errors: slides 1, 2, 4 and 7 are consistent.
