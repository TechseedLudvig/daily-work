# What a good final output contains

A report in the project's `outputs/`, in the structure of the August report: Energy, Cleaning,
Maintenance, Incidents, each with the month's figure and the change from the month before.

Must contain:
- Electricity 41 200 kWh, up 2 300 kWh (about 5.9%) from August's 38 900.
- District heating 12 400 kWh, up from August's 6 100.
- Cleaning complaints 2, down from 4.
- Maintenance tickets: 23 opened, 19 closed, so 4 open at month end (August: 17 opened, 17 closed).
- Incidents: the water leak in the basement storage on 22 September, and the lift outage on
  14 September, about six hours.
- Addressed to the property manager, as the August report is.

Must not contain:
- Sections the August report does not have.
- Figures that are not in the two documents, other than the lift outage.
