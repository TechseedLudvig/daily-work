# What a good final output contains

An email body in the project's `outputs/`, half a page at most.

Must contain:
- Decisions: we go with the vendor's hosted option; the pilot starts {{today+20d}} with 15 users.
- Actions with owner and due date: Jonas, security questionnaire, {{today+7d}}. Leila, list of
  the 15 pilot users, {{today+10d}}. The vendor (Deskline), draft contract, {{today+14d}}.
- Enough context that someone who was not in the meeting understands what was decided.

Must not contain:
- The price per agent, the 18% discount or the three-year term.
- A who-said-what account of the meeting.
