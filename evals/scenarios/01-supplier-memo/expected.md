# What a good final output contains

A memo in the project's `outputs/`, one page at most, in English, written for readers who are
not finance specialists.

Must contain:
- A clear recommendation: renew with Nordfrakt, or switch to a named supplier.
- Yearly cost at 18 400 pallets, or the difference against Nordfrakt (7 544 000 SEK):
  Baltic Cargo 8 188 000 SEK (644 000 more), Skanlog 7 912 000 SEK (368 000 more).
- The quality figures the recommendation rests on: on-time delivery 91%, 96%, 94% and damage
  claims 4.2, 2.1, 3.0 per 1000 shipments.
- Every figure traceable to the scorecard.

Good to have:
- Notice must be given by {{today+17d}} (60 days before {{today+77d}}), three days after the meeting.

Must not contain:
- Suppliers, prices or figures that are not in the scorecard.
