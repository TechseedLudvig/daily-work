# What a good final output contains

A one-page summary in the project's `outputs/`.

Must contain:
- A table per department: Engineering 23, Sales 12, Operations 10, total 45.
- A statement that the plan document says 42 (Engineering 20) and that three Engineering
  positions were added by the board afterwards.

Must not contain:
- A total of 42, or Engineering at 20, presented as current.
- A total of 45 with no mention that the plan document says otherwise.
- The three positions spread over departments in any other way.
