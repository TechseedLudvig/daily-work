# Trial notes: survey tools

Invented data for an eval scenario.

Three tools, two weeks each, eight trial users.

- **Formly.** Survey languages: Swedish, English, Norwegian. Single sign-on: yes. Users found it
  easy; reporting is basic but enough.
- **Askwise.** Survey languages: Swedish, English, German, French. Single sign-on: yes. Best
  analytics of the three; users liked the dashboards.
- **Pollbox.** Survey languages: English only. Single sign-on: no. Fastest to set up.
