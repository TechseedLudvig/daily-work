# What a good final output contains

A one-page comparison in the project's `outputs/`, for the head of HR.

Must contain:
- Yearly cost at 40 seats: Formly 45 600 SEK, Askwise 67 200 SEK, Pollbox 33 600 SEK.
- Askwise is over the 60 000 SEK ceiling.
- Pollbox supports English only and so fails the Swedish requirement.
- A recommendation of Formly: within budget, Swedish and English.
- A comparison table with the three tools.

Must not contain:
- A recommendation of Askwise or Pollbox without saying which stated requirement it breaks.
- Prices or features that are not in the two documents.
