# What a good final output contains

A checklist in the project's `outputs/`.

Must contain:
- A first-week checklist for a new hire in customer support, written for the team lead to tick off.
- Grouped by day, Monday to Friday, one page at most.
- Day 1: laptop and accounts. Days 2 and 3: shadowing a senior agent. Day 4: first own
  tickets with review. Day 5: feedback talk with the team lead.

Must not contain:
- Contract or payroll paperwork.
- A generic company-wide onboarding programme, or content for teams other than support.
